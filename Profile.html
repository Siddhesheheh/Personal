PRODUCT CASE STUDY · INSTAGRAM

Instagram — Intentional Usage

Instagram makes it easy to lose track of time. I researched why users experience unintended prolonged usage, then designed interventions that help them regain control without taking away intentional engagement.

  • Researched. Ran a quantitative survey with 26 users and 5 qualitative interviews to understand usage patterns, triggers and pain points.
  • Defined who it's for. Identified Passive Scrollers & Lurkers as the primary target persona.
  • Designed. Created an Intentional Usage experience with three interventions: Persistent Limit Re-entry Check, Take a Break and Non-Scrollable Shared Reel View.
  • Prioritized and measured. Prioritized problems using PIF scoring, defined success metrics and guardrails, then created user flows, edge cases and a PRD.
